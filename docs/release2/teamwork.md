# Teamwork

So far in the project, our team has focused on planning, coordination, and collaboration to ensure smooth progress and high-quality results, and we will continue to follow this approach.
From the beginning, we divided the project into clear tasks using GitHub Issues and assigned responsibilities based on each member’s interests and strengths.
Weekly physical meetings allowed us to synchronize progress, discuss challenges, and plan upcoming work.

Communication and collaboration have been central to our workflow.
We frequently discussed solutions in the group, which promoted knowledge sharing and early detection of errors.
Pair programming was used strategically, which strengthened both problem-solving skills and team cohesion.
Clear documentation and conventions, such as Conventional Commits, have helped maintain consistency throughout the development so far.

Following a structured workflow from task creation to branching, implementation, code review, and merging has ensured that everyone understood the process and expectations.
Code reviews have been particularly valuable for maintaining quality and identifying potential bugs or inconsistencies before integration.

We have not encountered many challenges, only a few minor ones.
This experience taught us the importance of listening to each member’s perspective and maintaining active communication.
But by addressing issues quickly and constructively, we were able to maintain momentum and a very positive team dynamic.

The project has so far strengthened our understanding of effective teamwork, communication, and project management.
We learned that clear planning and a supportive environment are key to achieving both technical and collaborative success.
These lessons will be valuable for future projects, where teamwork and coordination are crucial.
